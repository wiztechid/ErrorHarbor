Affiliate Disclosure

How ErrorHarbor uses affiliate links and keeps recommendations separate from troubleshooting guidance.

How affiliate links work

Some pages may contain links that can earn ErrorHarbor a commission if you make a purchase, at no extra cost to you.

Relevance rule

Affiliate offers should only appear where the product is contextually relevant to the problem, environment, prevention or migration path being discussed. A high payout alone is not a reason to recommend a product.

Editorial separation

Affiliate recommendations are visually separated from Quick Fixes, command blocks, warnings and verification steps. Affiliate relationships do not change whether a technical fix is presented as working or recommended.

Disclosure on pages

Pages containing affiliate links should also include a clear disclosure near the recommendation.
