Contact

Contact ErrorHarbor about technical corrections, site feedback, editorial questions or other inquiries.

Email us

The primary contact address for ErrorHarbor is info@errorharbor.com.

You can use this address for technical corrections, broken links, site feedback, editorial questions, accessibility issues, business inquiries or other matters related to ErrorHarbor.

Reporting a technical correction

To help us review a troubleshooting issue efficiently, include the URL of the affected page, the error or instruction you are referring to, and the product, operating system or software version when it is relevant. If you know of authoritative documentation that clarifies the issue, you may include its title or link.

Please describe what you observed rather than sending unnecessary system data. We may use reproducible reports to improve or clarify an existing guide.

Security and sensitive information

Do not email passwords, API keys, recovery codes, access tokens, private credentials, confidential logs or other secrets. Before attaching a screenshot or log excerpt, remove information that could identify an account, internal system or private resource unless it is genuinely necessary for the inquiry.

Editorial and general inquiries

For questions about our editorial approach, corrections process, attribution or general site matters, email the same address and use a clear subject line so the message can be reviewed in the right context.

Response expectations

Messages may be prioritized according to their impact on readers. Reports involving an unsafe instruction, materially incorrect troubleshooting step or broken core functionality may receive priority over general suggestions.
