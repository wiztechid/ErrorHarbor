# ErrorHarbor — 54-Article Content Audit (2026-10-29)

**Result:** Complete at repository and source-review level. This is an editorial/content audit, not an AdSense approval decision.

## Scope and method

- Audited all 54 indexable troubleshooting articles in the repository and matched each canonical path to the sitemap.
- Reviewed each article's title and intended error, opening diagnosis, Quick Check, branch headings, fix verification, safety guidance, internal related links, source section, visible review date, Article dateModified, and sitemap lastmod.
- Opened all **161 unique external reference URLs** found across the corpus; no URL failed to open in this check. Each article has 2–6 external references and at least two related internal links.
- Requested every production article URL once; all **54 returned HTTP 200**.
- Confirmed all 54 have a Quick Check, explicit verification guidance, source references, a visible review date matching Article dateModified, and a sitemap date matching Article dateModified. All have safety guidance; UptimeRobot uses “Do not silence the symptom” instead of a “What not to do” heading.
- First-party tests are recorded only where actually run. Six guides now include bounded Node.js/npm reproductions; UptimeRobot already has a separate recorded lab. Platform-specific fixes for Windows, Android, SQL Server/MySQL, VMware, printers, carrier networks, and managed servers were reviewed against their references but are not represented as tested here.
- No bulk rewrite or decorative-image addition was warranted. These technical guides use commands, decision branches, safety notes, and verification steps; add an image only when a real screenshot or diagram explains a step more clearly.

## Corpus findings

- Closely related pages route by different mechanisms: JavaScript identifier vs call target vs undefined receiver; npm PATH vs EACCES vs ERESOLVE vs OpenSSL; and SFTP chroot vs subsystem vs generic EOF.
- The mechanical crawl found no canonical, review-date, sitemap, source-count, Quick Check, verification, or related-link gaps.
- One stale npm CLI v8 reference was corrected to npm's current version-neutral Folders documentation in PR #38.
- The AdSense dashboard screenshot supplied on 2026-10-29 shows **Needs attention — Low value content**; it does not name a failing URL. This audit and its corrections do not guarantee approval. Keep resubmission on HOLD until the remaining live, Search Console, responsive, trust/legal, and account checks are complete.
- The article scan found no images. This is recorded, not treated as a defect. Generic images do not replace original diagnostics or evidence.
- The initial date-parity check compared the page header, Article `dateModified`, and sitemap `lastmod`; a later live QA also found a duplicate date in the article sidebar’s Guide Details. Three pages had stale sidebar dates (two npm guides and one MySQL guide). A correction is proposed in PR for this audit; after deployment, recheck that both visible dates match `dateModified`.
