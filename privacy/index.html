Privacy Policy

A plain-language overview of data ErrorHarbor may process when you use the site.

Last updated: September 2026.

Basic site use

ErrorHarbor is designed as a mostly static website. Standard web-server, CDN and security logs may process information such as IP address, browser type, device information, requested URL and timestamps for security, reliability and aggregate analytics.

Feedback and comments

Quick troubleshooting feedback may be stored using first-party infrastructure when that feature is enabled. Comments may use an external discussion provider such as Giscus/GitHub. Do not post passwords, API keys, recovery codes, tokens or other private credentials.

Advertising and affiliates

If advertising is enabled, advertising providers may process data according to their own policies and applicable consent requirements. Affiliate links can record that a referral originated from ErrorHarbor.

Cookies and consent

Cookie and consent controls will be implemented as required by the advertising and analytics services active on the site and the visitor’s region.

Changes

This policy will be updated when the site’s production analytics, feedback, advertising or comment systems materially change.
