Privacy Policy

A plain-language overview of data ErrorHarbor may process when you use the site.

Last updated: November 13, 2026.

Overview

ErrorHarbor is designed as a mostly static troubleshooting website. This policy explains information that may be processed when you use the site, contact us, use community features, follow affiliate links, or—when advertising is enabled—interact with advertising services.

Basic site use and technical logs

Web hosting, content delivery, security and related infrastructure may process technical information such as IP address, browser and device information, requested URL, timestamps and similar request data. This information may be used to deliver the site, maintain reliability, investigate abuse and understand aggregate site performance.

Contact and feedback

If you email info@errorharbor.com, we receive the information you choose to include in your message. Do not send passwords, API keys, recovery codes, access tokens, private credentials or unnecessary confidential logs. Troubleshooting feedback may also be stored using first-party site functionality when that feature is enabled.

Community features

Some troubleshooting pages may use an external discussion service such as Giscus, which is built on GitHub Discussions. If you choose to use a third-party community feature, that provider may process information under its own terms and privacy practices. Community participation is optional.

Advertising partners

ErrorHarbor may use Google AdSense and Adsterra to display advertising. Google, Adsterra and other advertising technology providers may process information for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and—where permitted and consented to—personalization. Adsterra ads are available only on troubleshooting guides and their scripts remain blocked until you choose “Allow ads.”

Advertising technologies may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or other identifiers. Third parties may place and read cookies in your browser, or use web beacons, IP addresses and similar technologies to collect information as a result of ad serving on ErrorHarbor.

For details, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Adsterra Privacy Policy.

Cookies, local storage and consent

ErrorHarbor may use cookies or local storage for site functionality and, when enabled, advertising or measurement. Where consent is required, an appropriate consent management mechanism will be used before relevant advertising or storage technologies are activated. Your available choices may vary by region and by the services active on the site.

Adsterra scripts do not load until a visitor explicitly chooses “Allow ads”; choosing “Keep ads off” prevents them from loading. This choice is stored in this browser and can be changed below. Google AdSense remains inactive until its separate Google AdSense Privacy & messaging configuration and consent flow are enabled. Where prior consent is required, including for relevant visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the Adsterra tags remain blocked until a choice is made. The available consent requirements and choices may vary by region and service.

Advertising choices

Use this control to review or change the Adsterra advertising choice saved in this browser. If you withdraw permission, ErrorHarbor stops loading Adsterra ads on later page views. You can also clear site data in your browser.

Google provides advertising controls that may allow users to manage or opt out of personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings. The choices available can depend on the user's account, browser, region and the advertising configuration active on ErrorHarbor.

Affiliate links

Some pages may contain clearly disclosed affiliate links. If you follow an affiliate link, the destination or affiliate network may receive referral information needed to attribute a visit or purchase. See our Affiliate Disclosure for our editorial approach to affiliate recommendations.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when production analytics, advertising, consent, feedback or community systems materially change. The “Last updated” date above identifies the current published version.

Contact

For privacy questions related to ErrorHarbor, email info@errorharbor.com.
